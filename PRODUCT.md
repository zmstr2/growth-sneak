# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Founders and growth leads at early-stage startups who want to understand how another company grows: a
competitor, a company they admire, or one they are about to pitch. They run `/sneak <url>` in Claude Code and read
the local HTML report on a laptop: once top to bottom, then again to look things up.

## Product purpose
Turn public signals into one report that answers "how does this company get customers, and what could I
borrow?", with proof a reader can click through to. Success: in 30 seconds a reader knows the company's strongest
growth plays; in 5 minutes they have seen the real posts, ads and pages behind them and picked a move to try.

## Brand personality
Clean, precise, quietly confident. A neutral tool that gets out of the way of the findings. Plain language, short
sentences, no hype.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards: stat-tile rows, chip soup, badges on everything.
- "AI report" look: every section the same weight, tiny grey labels, filler prose.
- Marketing theatrics: gradients, glass, decorative motion.

## Design principles
1. **Answer first.** The page opens with how the company grows and its strongest plays.
2. **Proof beside every claim.** A score sits next to the posts, ads and pages behind it.
3. **Same structure every time.** Fixed sections and activities, so reports compare.
4. **Easy to scan.** Name and score on the left, what we saw on the right; facts in labelled rows; sources as
   links at the foot.
5. **Honest by construction.** "Not covered" is visibly different from "checked, none found"; low confidence is
   said in words; estimates say they are estimates.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at least 4.5:1, no meaning carried by colour alone, keyboard use, visible focus, reduced
motion respected. Designed for desktop reading.
