# Voice

How every sentence in the report is written.

- **Plain words, short sentences.** If a smart 13-year-old would not follow it, rewrite it.
- **Phrases, not paragraphs.** One idea per line. Put the noun first. Cut filler ("really", "genuinely").
- **Direct, not clever, not salesy.** No "world-class", "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless". A superlative is
  allowed only when it is the company's own quoted words.
- **Say how we know.** Observed (we saw it) or stated (they said it). Never state what we could not check:
  write "we could not find", not "they do not".
- **No invented numbers.** Every figure comes from the evidence. Estimates (Similarweb traffic, Upspring ad spend)
  appear only with their source and the word "estimate"; never estimate revenue yourself.
- **No AI tells.** No stacked hedges, no rule-of-three padding, no vague attributions, no em-dash pile-ups.
  One honest hedge, not four.
- **No judgment words about the company** ("weak", "failing", "behind"). Describe what they do and how much.

## Length caps

| Field | Cap |
|---|---|
| Activity read | 16 words |

Calibration: "most of its growth is genuinely strong" becomes "most of its growth is strong".
