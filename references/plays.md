# Plays: moves a founder or growth lead can borrow

One card per activity. The analyst reads these when writing takeaways; the cards are not shown in the report.
A takeaway pairs what the company does (cited) with the move from the card, in the analyst's words. "Fits when"
and "Skip when" help decide whether the move is worth suggesting at all. A card is a move, not a promise: no
numbers about results unless they are the company's own stated figures.

Card shape (keep every line short):
- **Move.** One sentence, starts with a verb.
- **Why it works.** One sentence.
- **Fits when.** One line.
- **Skip when.** One line.

## Earned and social

### Company social (`organic_social`)
- **Move.** Post one useful thing a week in the style your buyers already read, and reuse the best into a thread or short video.
- **Why it works.** Regular posts keep you in front of people who are not ready to buy yet.
- **Fits when.** Your buyers are active on one platform you can name.
- **Skip when.** You cannot keep a weekly rhythm; a quiet account looks worse than none.

### Founder-led social (`founder_social`)
- **Move.** Have a founder post first-person stories about customers, mistakes and numbers, and let the company page reshare them.
- **Why it works.** People follow people. A founder's account usually reaches more than the brand's.
- **Fits when.** A founder is willing to write in public and has a story the audience wants.
- **Skip when.** Nobody on the team wants to own it; ghost-written posts read as ghost-written.

### Community (`community`)
- **Move.** Give users one place to ask each other questions, and have staff answer within a day.
- **Why it works.** Answers in public become proof for the next buyer and cut support load.
- **Fits when.** The product has users who learn from each other (developers, operators, creators).
- **Skip when.** You have fewer than a few hundred engaged users; an empty room does harm.

### Open source (`open_source`)
- **Move.** Release a small, genuinely useful tool or sample app, and keep it maintained.
- **Why it works.** Developers find and trust code they can read, and stars become public proof.
- **Fits when.** Your buyers are technical and can adopt a small piece without a sales call.
- **Skip when.** You would only release demos you will not maintain.

### Partnerships (`partnerships`)
- **Move.** Pick the one platform your customers already use and become its listed or preferred partner.
- **Why it works.** Their audience finds you inside a tool they already trust.
- **Fits when.** The partner platform has a directory or a partner programme you can apply to.
- **Skip when.** The partner could just as easily become a competitor.

### Press and podcasts (`press`)
- **Move.** Offer a founder to the podcasts and newsletters your buyers actually follow, with a specific story to tell.
- **Why it works.** A trusted host's introduction carries more weight than your own claim.
- **Fits when.** You have a real story, a number you can share, or a strong point of view.
- **Skip when.** You only have a product announcement.

### Events and webinars (`events`)
- **Move.** Run a small recurring session on a problem your buyers have, and record it.
- **Why it works.** Each session creates a live audience and a reusable asset.
- **Fits when.** You can teach something useful without it being a pitch.
- **Skip when.** You cannot fill a room; one good guest beats a lonely host.

### Video (`video`)
- **Move.** Publish short walkthroughs of real use cases, one problem per video.
- **Why it works.** Buyers who are evaluating want to see the product working before they talk to anyone.
- **Fits when.** The product is visual or the setup is hard to describe.
- **Skip when.** You cannot publish regularly.

### Referral and affiliate (`referral`)
- **Move.** Offer happy customers or partners a simple reward for introductions, with public terms.
- **Why it works.** Introductions from a peer convert better than cold outreach.
- **Fits when.** Customers already recommend you and the product has a clear value metric.
- **Skip when.** Your product is too new for customers to recommend yet.

## Owned

### Content and SEO (`seo_content`)
- **Move.** Publish pages for the questions buyers ask just before they buy, and update them on a schedule.
- **Why it works.** Search brings people who are already looking, and fresh pages keep ranking.
- **Fits when.** Your buyers search for answers before they buy.
- **Skip when.** Your market is too small or too new for anyone to be searching.

### AI-search readiness (`aeo`)
- **Move.** Make the site readable by AI assistants: allow their crawlers, publish an llms.txt, add structured data.
- **Why it works.** More buyers now ask an assistant first, and it can only cite what it can read.
- **Fits when.** You already have real content worth citing.
- **Skip when.** You have little content; fix that first.

### Comparison pages (`comparison`)
- **Move.** Write an honest page for each rival buyers compare you with, including who each one suits.
- **Why it works.** Evaluators search "X vs Y" and "alternatives to X" at the point of decision.
- **Fits when.** You sit in a category where buyers shortlist two or three tools.
- **Skip when.** You are in a category with no clear rivals.

### Customer proof (`customer_proof`)
- **Move.** Collect named customer stories with a result in the customer's own words.
- **Why it works.** Evaluators look for someone like them who succeeded.
- **Fits when.** You have a few customers willing to be named.
- **Skip when.** Customers cannot be named; use anonymised proof and say so.

### Ways to start (`signup_entry`)
- **Move.** Let a visitor reach the first useful result before they enter a card or talk to anyone.
- **Why it works.** People trust what they have tried, and every step before value loses some of them.
- **Fits when.** The product can show value in minutes.
- **Skip when.** Setup needs your team (security review, data migration); offer a guided trial instead.

### Pricing page (`pricing`)
- **Move.** Publish prices for the plans you can, and say what the price is based on.
- **Why it works.** Buyers who cannot find a price often leave instead of asking.
- **Fits when.** Most customers land on a standard plan.
- **Skip when.** Every deal is custom; say who it is for and what drives the price.

## Paid

### Google ads (`google_ads`)
- **Move.** Run ads on the searches that show buying intent, each pointing at a page written for that search.
- **Why it works.** You pay only when someone is already looking.
- **Fits when.** There is steady search demand for what you sell.
- **Skip when.** Few people search for your category yet.

### Paid social (`paid_social`)
- **Move.** Test a few different angles on one platform before adding another.
- **Why it works.** The platform shows which message works before you scale it.
- **Fits when.** You can reach your buyers by job title or interest.
- **Skip when.** You cannot yet say who the ad is for.
